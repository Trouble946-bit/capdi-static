About Our Organisation

At CAPDI (Caring for Persons with Disabilities), our mission is to systematically promote inclusiveness among socially excluded groups, empowering them to fully participate at every level of the decision-making process. By breaking down deep-rooted structural barriers across Malawi, we champion inclusive primary education, build accessible infrastructure like ramps and specialized facilities, and translate vital educational materials into Braille formats. Through strategic community advocacy and collaborative partnerships with local leaders, we actively dismantle social stigma to secure equal access to health, education, and justice frameworks, ensuring that every individual has the opportunity to thrive with dignity.

Mission

CAPDI is dedicated to advocating for and empowering women and girls with disabilities through education, awareness, and policy change, ensuring their voices are heard and their rights are upheld at local and national levels.

Vision

To create an inclusive and empowering society where marginalized women and girls, particularly those with disabilities, enjoy equal rights, opportunities, and participation in all aspects of life.

Values

Inclusion, Equality, Empowerment, Advocacy, Accountability, Collaboration & Respect for human rights.

Our Story

Caring for Persons with Disabilities (CAPDI) was founded in November 2004 by advocates committed to disability inclusion. Registered under the Trustees Incorporation Act (No. TR/INC: 2729), CAPDI promotes the rights and wellbeing of persons with disabilities, especially women and girls, through advocacy, empowerment, and service delivery.

Operation Bases

The organization serves communities across Malawi. Its secretariat is in Bangwe Township, Blantyre district, and it maintains a branch in Bangula, Nsanje district.

Our Team

Our executive team comprises highly qualified professionals with expertise in special needs education, language science, public health, accounting, and community development. They bring decades of institutional experience to deliver inclusive programming and gender-equitable community initiatives.
