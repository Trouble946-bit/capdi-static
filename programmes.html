Advocacy · Inclusion

01 · Nsanje, Malawi

Empowering Women & Girls with Disabilities

Women and girls are building confidence, claiming their rights and shaping the decisions that affect their lives.

The programme combines inclusive policy campaigns, community awareness, male-ally engagement, and evidence-based advocacy to make health, education, protection, and leadership spaces safe and accessible for all.

03 · Enterprise

Flower Vase Making

This initiative trains women in the production of flower vases, promoting entrepreneurship and sustainable income generation.

Key focus areas include teaching practical vase-making skills, enabling micro-business creation, and supporting local economic empowerment.

Community health

04 · Health & resilience

Soap Making Training

Supported by the U.S. Embassy and CDC guidance, this project trained communities in soap and mask production.

The programme improved hygiene and COVID-19 preparedness for 9,000 marginalized individuals by strengthening local production skills and safe-practice awareness.
